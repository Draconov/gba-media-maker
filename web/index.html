ROM and playback

Collections automatically use the media menu.

Playback: A pause/resume; B restart or menu; L/R previous/next media; Left/Right seek (video frame-step while paused, hold to repeat); Up/Down volume and SELECT mute only when the current media has audio; START cycles HUD; START+SELECT help. Manual images ignore A, and images/GIFs/silent videos have no audio controls. Collections with two or more items always open through the media menu.
